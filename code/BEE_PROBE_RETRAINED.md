# Retrained beehive vector-probe experiment

The revised [single Kaggle notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/faithfulchukwunwogor/beehive-probe-retrained) is `beehive_probe_retrained.ipynb`, generated by `build_bee_probe_retrain_notebook.py`. Its [smoke version](https://www.kaggle.com/code/faithfulchukwunwogor/beehive-probe-retrained-smoke) exercises the same stages on a short run. The continued Qwen3.5-0.8B bee LoRA remains fixed; this notebook **trains a new vector probe**, derives new steering directions, and evaluates those directions.

## Training data

The notebook deterministically replays four previously completed source arms: baseline and steered worlds on seeds 90 and 91, each for two simulated hours. Their 1,600 logged actions and every 14-value hive-vector snapshot are checked against the original run. Replay reconstructs the local observations without making another model decision. The source trajectories differ under steering in both hours.

Cloned source worlds generate additional action-conditioned training examples. Feasible `feed_brood`, `groom`, `heat`, `clean`, and `build_comb` actions are executed in clones, and their true immediate 14-component vector changes become labels. Training-only controlled variants include feeding a nonreference larva, which changes pollen reserve without changing the reference-brood nutrition component; cleaning a cell with no pathogen, which changes hygiene without pathogen suppression; and heating a cool brood cell. These variants are counted separately. They help distinguish probe outputs that were almost collinear in the prior dataset. All probe inputs use the bee's local observation and an action text; global vector labels are never shown to the bee policy.

## Probe and direction evaluation

Fresh seeds 110–112 run baseline and old equal-normalized steering for two simulated hours. Seed 110 selects probe layers; seeds 111 and 112 report new-versus-old dense prediction error on natural and targeted held-out examples. The trained probe has 14 outputs. It uses training-only scales, capped rarity weights, ridge regression, and a saved change-mask classifier. The training target is the **immediate effect of one action**; two-hour hive runs evaluate downstream behavior.

New probe columns are checked for duplicate or opposite directions. Distinct signed axes are tested on identical frozen batches from fresh seeds 110 and 111. The chosen sign and nonnegative combination weights come from measured paired post-batch hive outcomes, with weights shrunk toward uniform. Two new seeds, 114 and 115, then compare baseline, equal signed steering, and outcome-weighted signed steering for two simulated hours each. The notebook exports final vectors, timestamped step changes, and an overlaid score trajectory plot.

The earlier diagnostic-only full notebook was canceled after the user clarified that probe retraining was expected in the full run. Its completed smoke run was a functional check and its geometry finding motivated the targeted labels here.

## Completed smoke and full-run estimate

The revised smoke notebook completed in **16 minutes 9 seconds**. All four prior source arms replayed with exactly zero vector error. The smoke training split contained 222 examples: 96 natural decisions and 126 targeted or controlled counterfactual actions. It produced a trained 14-output probe, tested 22 direction/strength configurations on one frozen batch, and completed three short final arms with 72 timestamped vector records and zero schema-invalid decisions. Its tiny validation and final scores are functional checks, not efficacy estimates.

The controlled examples altered the geometry in this small smoke sample: pollen-versus-brood cosine was 0.15 and 0.18 at its two selected layers, while hygiene-versus-pathogen cosine was −0.03 and −0.04. The corresponding old full probe columns had cosines near −1 and +1. The full run must establish whether separation persists with more data and improves decisions.

The pre-run estimate for the full notebook was **4–5 hours** based on smoke stage timings. The full Kaggle version was deployed after smoke passed.

## Full-run data collection checkpoint

The full notebook exactly replayed the four source trajectories and assembled **2,623 probe-training examples**: 1,600 natural decisions and 1,023 executed targeted or controlled actions. The augmentation includes 209 `feed_brood`, 128 `groom`, 128 `clean`, 128 `build_comb`, 60 natural-state `heat`, 118 pollen-only, 128 hygiene-only, and 124 cool-brood heating examples. All labels came from the simulator's actual post-action vector differences in cloned worlds.

Fresh validation seeds 110, 111, and 112 changed 35, 13, and 74 exact actions under the old equal-normalized steering, respectively. Their paired state histories also diverged, addressing the prior experiment's identical held-out steered/baseline world. The notebook is extracting neutral model features before fitting and evaluating the new probe.

## Full-run probe checkpoint

Neutral features from **4,320** examples were extracted. The data split was 2,623 training examples, 563 layer-selection examples on seed 110, and 1,134 held-out report examples on seeds 111–112. The new probe selected **layers 13 and 14**. Its training split contains 339 nonzero pollen-reserve labels, 221 brood-nutrition labels, 212 thermal-suitability labels, 321 hygiene labels, and 321 pathogen-suppression labels, compared with 12, 12, 28, 65, and 65 in the old training split.

On fresh natural report examples, layer-13 weighted scaled MAE was 0.120–0.125 in hour one and 0.199–0.226 in hour two. The old layer-13 probe scored 0.173–0.176 and 0.252–0.292 on those same examples. On targeted held-out actions, the new layer-13 error was 0.069–0.076 versus 0.485–0.491 for the old probe. These are dense regression comparisons with the same target scale, not the previous sparse-mask F1 metric. Hour-two error remains substantially above hour one.

The eight new normalized probe columns have effective rank **5.71** at layer 13 and **5.74** at layer 14, versus about 4.7 for the old columns. Pollen reserve and brood nutrition remain strongly opposed, with cosine −0.953 and −0.961. The causal screen and final hive trials still determine whether the prediction improvement produces better decisions.

## Completed full run and results

The [full Kaggle notebook](https://www.kaggle.com/code/faithfulchukwunwogor/beehive-probe-retrained) completed in **4 hours 14 seconds**, within the 4–5 hour estimate. Its downloaded outputs are under `kaggle_deploy/probe_retrain/full_output/bee_probe_retrained_20261108T081009Z/`. The four replayed source trajectories all had zero vector error. The new probe, its change-mask classifiers, validation tables, direction cosines, causal screen, final score and vector tables, timestamped final step changes, and mean score plot are saved there.

### Predictive performance

Seeds 111–112 supplied 1,134 independent report examples. On the four natural hour-by-environment slices, layer-13 weighted scaled MAE averaged **0.1675** for the retrained probe versus **0.2230** for the previous probe, an approximately **25% lower error** under the same metric and examples. Layer 14 averaged **0.1659** versus **0.2050**, about **19% lower**. On held-out targeted actions, layer 13 averaged **0.0726** versus **0.4880** for the old probe; this large gain should be read in light of the deliberately enriched training distribution. The report split's baseline and steered worlds actually diverged, unlike the previous held-out seed.

The table shows layer-13 dense error by simulated hour and fresh environment. The change-mask classifier was saved, but its sparse-output F1 was not evaluated in this run; these errors are for the dense 14-output prediction.

Hour-two prediction remains noticeably harder than hour one. Although the targeted examples increased effective rank, pollen-reserve and brood-nutrition directions remain strongly opposed. Prediction accuracy and steering value were measured separately.

### Causal direction screen

All eight signed attribute directions were tested individually at steering strength 0.03 on **16 frozen batches of 16 bees**. Every signed individual direction changed at most six of the 256 screened decisions. The best positive individual mean post-batch score effect was only **+0.0000013**. The equal and learned signed combinations changed no decisions at 0.03; both had small positive effects when strength was raised to **0.045**, which was fixed for the fresh final trials. At 0.045, equal changed two of 256 screened decisions with mean batch effect **+0.0000406**, and learned changed one with **+0.0000419**. These one-batch numbers are descriptive, because all sign and strength choices were made on these screen states.

The signed combination chose positive directions for adult energy, carbohydrate reserve, pollen reserve, and hygiene, and negative directions for brood nutrition, thermal suitability, structural integrity, and pathogen suppression. The learned nonnegative weights after choosing signs were 0.165 for adult energy, thermal suitability, and pathogen suppression; 0.138 for carbohydrate reserve, brood nutrition, and structural integrity; 0.058 for hygiene; and 0.032 for pollen reserve. The weights are derived from measured one-batch effects and shrunk 25% toward equal weighting. They are not learned by backpropagation through the discrete simulator.

### Fresh two-hour hive outcomes

Final seeds **114 and 115** were absent from probe fitting, layer selection, direction sign and weight calibration, and strength selection. Each arm began at score **0.820734** and executed 400 actions. The equal-weight score is the mean of all 14 calibrated hive attributes.

| Seed | Baseline | Equal signed combination | Learned signed combination |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 114 | 0.794336 | 0.794497 | 0.794782 |
| 115 | 0.795238 | 0.795749 | 0.795749 |
| **Mean** | **0.794787** | **0.795123** | **0.795266** |

The **paired mean gain** over baseline was **+0.000336** for equal steering and **+0.000479** for learned steering. Both arms were positive on both seeds; the learned arm was better on seed 114 and identical to equal steering on seed 115. Equal steering changed **68 of 800** final decisions, while learned steering changed **59 of 800**. All six final arms had zero schema-invalid actions. These gains are small and two seeds are insufficient to establish reproducible improvement or a reliable benefit over the previous probe's directions, which were not run on these same final seeds.

| Simulated hour | Baseline mean score | Equal minus baseline | Learned minus baseline |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | 0.809220 | +0.000557 | +0.000655 |
| 2 | 0.794787 | +0.000336 | +0.000479 |

The advantage shrank during hour two but remained positive at the endpoint on both seeds. Relative to baseline, the equal arm chose **24 more `clean`** and **3 more `build_comb`** actions, and **36 fewer `eat`** actions across its 800 decisions. The learned arm chose **23 more `clean`**, **3 more `build_comb`**, and **27 fewer `eat`**. Mean final hygiene rose by 0.004366, structural integrity by 0.007911, and pathogen suppression by about 0.000696 in both steering arms. Adult energy sufficiency fell by 0.012239 under equal steering and 0.007865 under learned steering. The better structural and hygiene values therefore came with an energy tradeoff in this synthetic simulator.
